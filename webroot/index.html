Tuning

70

How much of the cleaned mic signal to blend in (wet/dry).

60

Blend for output cleanup. These are speech models — lower this if music sounds over-processed.

24 dB

Caps how aggressively noise is attenuated (protects natural tone).

50

Voice-activity sensitivity used for gating and auto-gain.

High-pass filter

Removes DC offset and low rumble.

Auto gain

Gently boosts quiet speech (may pump in noise).
